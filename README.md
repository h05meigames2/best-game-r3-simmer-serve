**Simmer & Serve** — a dinner-rush kitchen arcade. You are the chef of a small
restaurant: tickets arrive with a countdown, and every dish is built by hand.
Grab ingredients from the pantry, chop them on the board, grill patties and
simmer soup on the stove, plate the dish, and serve it through the window
before the guest walks out. Wash the dirty plates at the sink or the plating
counter runs dry — and never leave a patty on the stove too long, or it burns.

Three services, each harder than the last: more tickets at once, shorter
patience, and a new dish on the menu (Garden Salad, Classic Burger, Tomato
Soup, and the five-part Double Stack). Clear a service's coin target to pick
one upgrade — Swift Shoes, an Extra Burner, or a Calmer Dining Room — then
face the next rush. Three walk-outs, or closing time with the target unmet,
ends the night.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 — no build step, no network requests, just
static files under `game/`. Move with WASD or arrow keys, press E (or click a
station) to use it, P to pause, M to mute. All art is drawn in code and all
sound is synthesized with WebAudio.
